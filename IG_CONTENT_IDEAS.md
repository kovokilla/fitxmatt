# IG Content Creation Ideas
Source: @therealbrianmark breakdown (Business Coach for Online Trainers)
Added: 2026-07-25

## Formats + performance notes (verbatim from Brian Mark)

### 1. Long form clip ups — WORST
- Worst type of content to post on social media because 99% of people don't know what to clip, and as a result, the content just flops.

### 2. Regular talking heads — FLOPS without graphics
- Used to be the most viral content on social media, but as soon as everybody started using a high quality camera to do these, they flopped.
- **Fix:** Include graphics on screen while talking head — makes it more visually appealing, retention higher.

### 3. Whiteboard breakdowns — HIGH VALUE
- Great style of content to do deep value videos with because you can attach a visual with the information that you're delivering.

### 4. Client result videos — LOW ENG, HIGH CONV
- Will get you the least amount of engagement on social media, but by far will sign you the most amount of clients.
- Example: "This one video right here I found my client at $142,000 — caused me to sign three high ticket clients the next day."

### 5. AI carousels — HIGHEST VIRAL POTENTIAL (no sales)
- One of favorite types of content right now for getting more views.
- It's not going to lead to any sales, but has one of the highest chances of going viral right now.

### 6. Ranking videos — HIGH RETENTION
- By nature, they create a curiosity gap and make people wanna stick around until the end of the video so they can find out what number one is.

### 7. Personal story carousels — EASY + RELATABLE
- One of the easiest types of content to create because you're just basing it off of your life story.
- Has a high chance of going viral because it's widely relatable to a large group of people.

### 8. Clone videos — #1 VIRAL CHANCE
- By far, this type of video right now has the highest chance of going viral.
- Quick clone videos (quick value) or in-depth clone videos (deep value).

### 9. Raw talk videos — #1 AUTHENTICITY
- Number one in 2026 because people are craving authenticity.
- Open up your phone and speak to the camera = lots of views and engagement.

---
## FitXMatt take / applicability
- Client result videos = high conversion for a coach (even though eng is lower). FitXMatt has real client results (Slavo, Vladimir, etc.) — these belong in the feed, not the carousel bank.
- AI carousels + personal story carousels = top virality play when you want reach.
- Raw talk + whiteboard breakdown = best for deep value IT-domain videos.
- Ranking videos (e.g., "Top 5 reasons IT pros can't lose weight") fit the listicle frame already used in Post 9.
